About

Biblical. Practical. Reproducible.

The ACPT training pathway uses the Four Fields framework—a biblical approach to disciple-making and church planting rooted in the practices of the early church.

Each training builds on the previous, walking participants through gospel conversations, discipleship, and church planting.

We exist to serve the church by training believers to make disciples who make disciples.
